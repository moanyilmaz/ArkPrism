\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ArkPrism makes privacy-source binding explicit in ArkTS static analysis. It resolves a platform operation, its data-carrying value, and the witness for both decisions across package exports, manager receivers, executable properties, IR aliases, callbacks, Promise continuations, and \texttt{await}. The resulting binding is preserved through IFDS and attached to each audit path.

The evaluation shows the value of this design at complementary levels. ArkPrism classifies all 186 source-first real-project candidates, increases HapBench specificity by 28.57 percentage points and F1 by 2.65 points over HapFlow, isolates nine Promise paths to continuation-carrier transfer, and classifies all 24 Promise semantic mutations. Manual review confirms all 90 sampled privacy-data paths, and the analysis completes \CorpusProjects{} projects without resource fallback. These results establish evidence-carrying source binding as a practical foundation for precise OpenHarmony privacy analysis.
